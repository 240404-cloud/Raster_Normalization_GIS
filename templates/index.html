RASTER INTELLIGENCE

More than a download button.

RasterNormalize GIS turns preprocessing into a transparent workflow: the application detects the input, standardizes image data where needed, measures its distribution, applies a reproducible transformation and presents the result for visual inspection.

METHODOLOGY

Scientifically transparent by design.

01

Format-aware

GeoTIFF spatial metadata is preserved; ordinary images are clearly marked non-georeferenced.

02

Statistics-first

Valid pixels, range, mean and variation are calculated before transformation.

03

Reproducible

Min-Max normalization maps valid values consistently to the 0–1 interval.
